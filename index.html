All inputs are pre-tax annual values. TVOG, expenses, strain, and underwriting profit are amounts in the same unit as L; ALM is a rate. NAi is allocated from C-ROSS required capital, not reserve size.

Model scope

What this model proves—and what it does not

  • It shows how a C-ROSS risk-based allocation changes capital intensity, capital cost, required yield, and product ranking.
  • TVOG, expenses, underwriting profit, new-business strain, and ALM are now captured as editable annual pre-tax inputs. Cash-flow timing and company-level diversification allocation still require a multi-period valuation layer before use in pricing or performance management.
  • Use after-tax or pre-tax terms consistently. This model converts shareholder ROE to pre-tax before comparing it with the pre-tax SAA yield.
